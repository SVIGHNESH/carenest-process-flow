Through each person's eyes

What each person does and what they hear about.

Family · CareNest appBooks care for themselves or a family member.
  1. Sign in

    One code sent to your phone.

  2. Book a visit

    Pick the service, day and time, and a nurse you'd like. Longer care is sold as daily shifts, like 8 or 12 hours a day.

  3. Agree and pay

    See the cancellation rule and tick to agree. Your payment is held, not taken, until a nurse says yes.

  4. Wait for a nurse

    Usually minutes. If no nurse can come, the held payment goes back to you by itself.

  5. Get confirmed

    An SMS with the nurse's name and the date. Only now is the payment confirmed.

  6. On the day

    Give the nurse the visit code from the app so the visit can start.

  7. Cancel if you need to

    The app shows the exact amount coming back before you confirm. Full refund before a nurse says yes or more than 12 h before the visit, 50% from 3 to 12 h, nothing in the last 3 h.

CareNest team · Admin dashboardRuns the service and steps in when something is stuck.
  1. Set up

    Add services with a price per hour, and add nurses.

  2. Watch bookings live

    Every booking shows up as it happens. Nothing needs approving.

  3. Step in when stuck

    If no nurse says yes within 1 hour, you're alerted and can assign a nurse by hand.

  4. Handle emergencies

    Give a full refund whatever the timing if the patient is taken to hospital, and note why.

  5. Fix failed refunds

    Refunds start by themselves. If one fails, you're alerted to sort it out.

Nurse · Nurse appVisits the patient at home.
  1. First login

    Use the email and temporary password from the CareNest team, then set your own password.

  2. Set your schedule

    Mark leave days and working hours.

  3. Get an offer

    A notification with the service, time and area. You have 15 minutes to accept or decline. If you decline, the next nurse is asked.

  4. Before the visit

    See the patient's details, condition and the address on a map.

  5. Visit

    Enter the family's code to start, and tap Complete when you finish.

  6. Get paid

    CareNest pays your share after the visit, for example weekly. A late cancel by the family still pays you a visit fee.
