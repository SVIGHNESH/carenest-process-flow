How CareNest works

Three apps, one booking, and what each person sees.

Through each person's eyes

What each person does and what they hear about.

Family · CareNest appBooks care for themselves or a family member.
  1. Sign up

    Name, phone, email and a password. A code goes to your email and your phone; enter both to get in.

  2. Browse services

    Every service shows its price per hour and a short description.

  3. Book a visit

    Pick the day and start time, how many hours or days, and a nurse who is free then.

  4. Add patient details

    Booking for yourself? Your name and phone are used. Booking for someone else? Add them as a family member with their name, age, sex and a contact phone; they're saved for next time. Add any condition or notes for the nurse.

  5. Review and pay

    Add the address (saved or new, with a map pin), check the total, then pay securely online. The booking shows "Waiting for confirmation".

  6. Get confirmed

    An SMS tells you the nurse and the date.

  7. During the visit

    SMS when the nurse starts and when the visit is complete.

  8. Manage bookings

    See current bookings and history. Cancel any time before the visit starts for a full refund. Keep family members and saved addresses in your profile.

CareNest team · Admin dashboardRuns the service and approves every booking.
  1. Set up

    Add services with a price per hour, and add nurses with a temporary password.

  2. Get alerted

    Every paid booking appears on the dashboard, with an SMS and an email to the team.

  3. Confirm or decline

    Keep the family's chosen nurse or pick another free one. Declining needs a reason and refunds the family automatically.

  4. Keep bookings on track

    Reassign the nurse, add notes, or cancel with a refund.

  5. Check nurses

    See each nurse's leave days and working days.

Nurse · Nurse appVisits the patient at home.
  1. First login

    Use the email and temporary password from the CareNest team, then set your own password.

  2. Set your schedule

    Mark leave days and working hours. A day with no entry counts as available.

  3. Get assigned

    An SMS and an email with the booking, service, time, patient and address.

  4. Before the visit

    See the patient's name, age, sex, condition, contact phone and the address on a map.

  5. Visit

    Tap Start when you arrive and Complete when you finish. The family and the team are told.

  6. Changes

    If a visit is cancelled you get an SMS and it leaves your list.

Gets told when
