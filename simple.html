How CareNest works

From booking a nurse at home to a finished visit, in six steps.

books and pays

NurseGets the visit details, visits, and completes it.

One booking, start to finish

You get an SMS at every step that matters.

  1. 1
    You

    Create your account

    Sign up with your name, phone and email. We send a code to both. Enter the codes to verify and you're in.

  2. 2
    You

    Choose your care

    Pick a service, the date and time, how long you need, a nurse who is free, who the care is for (you or a family member), and the address.

  3. 3
    You

    Pay securely

    Check the total and pay through Razorpay.

    SMS: request received
  4. 4
    CareNest team

    We confirm your booking

    Our team reviews every paid booking. We confirm your chosen nurse, or assign another free nurse.

    SMS: confirmed, with the nurse and date
  5. 5
    Nurse

    The nurse visits

    The nurse gets the visit details by SMS and email, arrives at your address and starts the visit.

    SMS: visit started
  6. 6
    Nurse

    Visit complete

    The nurse marks the visit as done. It moves to your booking history.

    SMS: visit completed
